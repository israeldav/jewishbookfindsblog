Jewish Book Finds brings together photographs, notes, and references from visits to historic Jewish sites and from items in my collection. It serves as a place to document material worth preserving, organizing, and sharing.

Topic Hubs

Browse the main areas of the site.

Jewish Cemeteries

Historic cemetery visits, directions, notes, and related research, with room to expand into early colonial cemeteries and other subtopics.

Old Synagogues

Synagogue buildings, local history, and site visits focused on older American Jewish communities.

Hebrew Sefarim

Siddurim, chumashim, and other Hebrew sefarim from different places, periods, and print traditions.

Jewish History Books

Books and printed material tied to modern Jewish history, especially unusual or worthwhile items from my collection.

Ephemera

Pamphlets, institutional printed material, and other pieces that do not fit neatly into the book categories.
